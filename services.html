The six traps, free, right now

These cost us hours each. Take them whether you hire us or not:

  1. Arc mainnet has no allowlist on the write path.
  2. 20 gwei is a protocol floor, not a suggestion — below it a transaction can sit pending indefinitely.
  3. The gas token and the bridged token share one balance. Read-balance → approve → transfer-that-balance reverts, because the read already counted the fee you were about to burn. The retry makes it strictly worse: a failed attempt is still a debit.
  4. DEX indexers see an Arc pair only after its first swap. A live pool is invisible to every router until then.
  5. token0() returns a 32-byte padded address. Comparing it to a bare checksummed address inverts your reserves and prints nonsense prices.
  6. explorer.arc.io is behind Cloudflare, so contract verification must be submitted from a browser.
