What this tool does — and does not do

It sends and receives

NUR is a standard ERC-20, so a payment is an ordinary token transfer you approve in your own wallet.

Everything is on chain

Balances, price and reserves come from eth_call against Arc. Nothing is cached or invented here.

It holds nothing

There is no server, no account and no key handling. Your wallet signs; this page only prepares the request.

It cannot make NUR valuable

A payment tool makes a currency spendable. Whether anyone wants to spend it is a separate question, and no tool answers it.

Need USDC on Arc to pay gas? Arc has no faucet — gas is real USDC. Bridge it with Circle CCTP from Base, Ethereum or Arbitrum, or withdraw from an exchange that lists Arc.
Noor Pay is a static page. No server, no analytics, no cookies. It is not affiliated with Circle, Arc, or Uniswap.
Nothing here is financial advice. Crypto assets can lose all value.
