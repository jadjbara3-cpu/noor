What Noor is

A fixed-supply digital currency, and nothing it does not claim to be.

Fixed supply

1,000,000,000 NUR. The cap is set in the constructor and cannot be changed — not by the owner, not by anyone. remainingMintable() returns zero.

No inflation, ever

The only way supply grows is by re-issuing tokens that were previously burned. Total supply can never exceed one billion.

Verifiable source

The Solidity source is published on the Arc explorer and matches the deployed runtime bytecode exactly — an exact-match verification, not a partial one.

Gasless approvals

EIP-2612 permit lets you approve a spender with a signature instead of a transaction. No gas to approve.

Burnable

Any holder can destroy their own tokens, permanently reducing supply. Deflation is available to everyone, not just the issuer.

Bounded issuer powers

Fees default to zero and can never exceed 5%. Ownership transfers need two steps. Ownership can be renounced to freeze every owner power forever.

How to get NUR

Three ways, in order of how little they cost you.

  1. Swap on Uniswap (Arc). The NUR/USDC pair is live on Uniswap v2 on Arc. Open the Uniswap app with the token address below, or inspect the pair on the explorer.
  2. Receive a transfer. NUR is a standard ERC-20 — anyone can send it to your Arc address from any wallet that supports Arc.
  3. Bridge USDC to Arc, then swap. Arc has no faucet. Circle's CCTP moves USDC from Base, Ethereum, Arbitrum and others to Arc natively.
